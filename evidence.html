How to interpret the comparisonNDVI is a satellite-derived indicator of vegetation greenness. Compare similar seasons and inspect cloud, land-cover and crop-calendar context. This page provides a side-by-side visual comparison of NASA's rendered composites; it does not calculate a pixel-by-pixel NDVI difference or identify crop types, yield, irrigation need or farm-level health. If a date has no available processed image, its map may show only the basemap.

What a visual change can and cannot mean

Visible patternPossible interpretationWhat to check before concluding
Greener appearanceHigher vegetation greenness in the displayed composite.Season, crop calendar, rainfall, irrigation, land-cover change and image quality.
Less-green appearanceLower vegetation greenness in the displayed composite.Harvest timing, fallow fields, drought, cloud contamination and land-cover differences.
Little visible differenceNo obvious visual change at this map scale.Subtle changes may not be distinguishable in rendered map tiles.
