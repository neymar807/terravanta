What this view can tell you

Broad patterns such as vegetation cover, bare ground, settlement, water and cloud cover may be visible. Individual fields may be too small to distinguish at this scale.

Important: This first version displays true-colour imagery, not a calculated NDVI or crop-condition score. It must not be interpreted as a quantitative vegetation-health measurement.

Understanding the three model layers

The values above are average model estimates across the forecast hours, not readings from three probes in a real field.

Soil moisture limitationThese values come from weather-model estimates, not a sensor in the field. They are not a soil test, do not describe every farm equally, and should not be used alone to decide when or how much to irrigate. Compare them with field observations and local measurements.

How to interpret this dashboardHot-day screening uses a simple 35°C daily maximum threshold; low-rain screening uses less than 5 mm total over seven days; high-gust screening uses 40 km/h. These are illustrative watch thresholds for this prototype, not validated local agricultural risk thresholds. Local advisories, crop stage, soil, irrigation access and farm records matter.
