What the indicators mean

Heat pressureTemperature extremes and duration

A proper assessment would examine daily maximum temperature, warm-spell duration, crop growth stage and locally relevant thresholds.

Rainfall variabilityTiming and variation

A proper assessment would compare rainfall totals and timing with a suitable local baseline, including dry spells and seasonal distribution.

Water contextSupply and access

A proper assessment needs local irrigation access, groundwater or surface-water context, soil properties and farm records. Satellite or gridded rainfall alone cannot confirm farm water availability.

How the prototype index is formed

For this interface demonstration only, the three sample scores are averaged equally:

Index = (Heat + Rainfall + Water) ÷ 3

The display rounds the result to the nearest whole number. Equal weighting is a design placeholder, not a scientifically validated weighting scheme. A future research version must document thresholds, baseline periods, uncertainty and validation with local experts.

What to verify locally

  • Recent field temperature and rainfall records.
  • Soil texture, depth, salinity and water-holding properties.
  • Irrigation reliability and seasonal water access.
  • Crop type, planting date and sensitive growth stages.
  • Advice from local agricultural extension staff or qualified agronomists.
