How to interpret the comparisonCompare the direction and size of changes within each region. Temperature is the mean of valid daily T2M values; precipitation is the sum of valid daily PRECTOTCORR values. These are gridded regional estimates, not farm measurements. Differences between regions do not by themselves prove climate change attribution or crop impacts.
How to read this evidenceThese are historical gridded environmental estimates from NASA POWER, not measurements taken on your individual field. They can help describe regional conditions, but cannot by themselves determine soil health, irrigation needs, or the best crop rotation.

What TerraVanta does not doIt does not predict future weather, diagnose soil, calculate yield, or prescribe crop rotations. A responsible rotation comparison needs verified local soil information, crop characteristics, and advice from qualified local agricultural sources. These are planned next steps, not claims about the current prototype.
