How this indicator worksFor each month, TerraVanta compares rainfall in the selected year with the average rainfall for that same calendar month in the other five years. Below 75% is flagged as unusually low; below 50% is flagged as very low. This is a simple rainfall-anomaly screen, not the Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI), an official drought declaration, or a field-level drought measurement. Interpret it with local observations and a longer climate record.
Data limitationsNASA POWER provides gridded regional estimates, not measurements from an individual farm. The available comparison period on this page is 2019–2024. Missing or incomplete daily records can affect annual and monthly summaries. This page does not forecast weather or prescribe crops or irrigation.
